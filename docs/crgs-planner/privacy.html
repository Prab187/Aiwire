CRGS Connect Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

CRGS Connect is a planner for students. It keeps your timetable, homework, bag checklist, notes, school contacts and game scores so you can use them each day.

What we collect

Nothing. We do not collect, receive or store any of your information. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no adverts, no analytics and no tracking.

Where your information is kept

Everything you type into the app (timetable, homework, notes, contacts, scores and settings) is saved only on your own iPhone. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. If you delete the app, this information is deleted with it.

The Radio tab and the internet

The Radio tab downloads public news headlines about AI in schools from free news services (GDELT, BBC News and Schools Week). To do this, the app sends a search for news. It does not send your name, your timetable, your homework or anything else you type, and the only school name it sends is the one set under "Tune the radio", which starts as Colchester Royal Grammar School and which you can change or clear. Like any web request, the news service can see your device's IP address. We do not receive this and cannot see it.

Headlines are read aloud by your iPhone's built-in voice, on the device. Stories that mention sensitive topics are left out, but the headlines come from third parties, so check a story before relying on it. Schools and AI names are spotted automatically and can be wrong. Tapping "Read the story" opens the publisher's website in your browser, which has its own privacy policy.

Third parties

The app has no third-party tracking or advertising. The only third parties it talks to are the news services above, and only to download headlines.

Children

The app is designed to be safe for children. It asks for no personal information and contains no purchases or adverts. The only links are "Read the story" buttons on news headlines, which open the publisher's website.

Not affiliated with the school

CRGS Connect is an independent student app. It is not made, run or endorsed by Colchester Royal Grammar School.
