MyDayAtSchool Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

MyDayAtSchool is a planner for students. It keeps your timetable, homework, bag checklist, notes, school contacts and game scores so you can use them each day.

What we collect

Nothing. We do not collect, receive or store any of your information. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no adverts, no analytics and no tracking.

Where your information is kept

Everything you type into the app is saved only on your own iPhone. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. If you delete the app, this information is deleted with it.

Third parties

The app does not use any third-party services or share information with anyone.

Children

The app is designed to be safe for children. It asks for no personal information, has no links to other websites and contains no purchases or adverts.

Not affiliated with any school

MyDayAtSchool is an independent app. It is not run, endorsed or approved by any school.
